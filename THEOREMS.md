# Theorem and evidence map

This publication-facing map records the scope of the manuscript. Independent
AI-session reviews reconstructed the analytic arguments; these are not human
peer review, full formal verification, or a certification of novelty.

| Manuscript statement | Argument and attribution | Exact scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Theorem 1: two-polar Schur compression | Simultaneous Gaussian control and two polar normalizations | Existence of one fixed square unitary, independent rank-r mixed initializer, full physical dimension charged, arbitrary reference; no efficient synthesis claim. The square-root Kraus-rank ceiling is known from mixed-environment rank counting. |
| Theorem 2: entropy converse | Araki-Lieb and finite-dimensional entropy continuity | General square implementation, normalized Choi state, half diamond error with explicit continuity correction. |
| Theorem 3: exact extreme cost | Extremality and minimal Stinespring uniqueness | Exact only: m >= k rank(beta). Mixed spectators are permitted and charged. The qualitative obstruction for extreme channels has earlier precedent. |
| Lemma 4: ququart example | Haagerup-Musat Example 3.2, in the stated adjoint convention, plus Choi's criterion | Explicit s=1/3 Kraus pair, independently checked product independence and extremality, Gram rank equal to Kraus rank, flat Choi spectrum. |
| Corollary 5: exact/approximate tensor-power separation | The preceding construction and converses | All T inputs jointly available, error T^-4 for T >= 2, arbitrary fixed hardware; initial apparatus rather than payload custody. |
| Lemma 6 and Theorem 7: support inverse and epoch bound | Transposed Kraus products, a stabilized linear inverse, and one passive tester | Mandatory arrivals, matching physical release schedules, one residual, all outward system factors counted. The linear inverse need not be positive. |
| Sections 7-8: ququart inverse and delay curve | Tetrahedral reconstruction and the epoch bound | Sublogarithmic-delay consequence at error T^-4; logarithmic curve is a lower bound, not a proved optimal frontier. Norm 3 is optimal among linear inverse extensions, not a sharp physical delay claim. |
| Theorem 8: delayed independent blocks | Independent initial stock and retained used apparatus | Mandatory arrivals, outputs at block ends, no unfinished-block flush, no new supplies after time zero. Peak payload adds 2b qubits. |

## Unproved extensions excluded from the manuscript

- General channels in the upper construction; efficient uniform gate synthesis.
- Caller-paced inputs, optional arrivals, early flushing, autonomous timing.
- Equality of the critical logarithmic-delay lower curve and an optimum.
- General public-record or larger output-carrier resource extensions.
- Full Lean verification of any headline analytic or asymptotic theorem.
